AI Guard

On-device hand tracking

Watches your webcam for a hand lingering near your hair and interrupts you automatically — before you've consciously decided to pull.

Everything runs locally in this browser tab using Google's MediaPipe hand + face tracking. No video is ever recorded, stored, or sent anywhere — the camera feed never leaves your device. The preview below is shown so you can always see exactly what it sees.

AI Guard is off
First activation downloads a small on-device AI model (a few MB, one time only) — needs internet once, then works offline. Requires camera access, and this browser tab has to stay open in front of you (a phone screen that's locked or a background tab can't see anything, by design).

Trichyguard stores everything only on this device, in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, tracked, or shared — not even to us. If you ever want a clean slate, "Erase all my data" is permanent and immediate.

Trichyguard is a self-help companion for hair-pulling (trichotillomania) and related body-focused repetitive behaviors. It is not a substitute for therapy — habit reversal training with a licensed therapist (often called HRT or ComB) is the most evidence-backed treatment. The TLC Foundation for BFRBs is a good place to find one if you're looking.
